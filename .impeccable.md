## Design Context

### Users
Brussels social organisations with a communication, design, or digital
challenge, and volunteer makers who contribute creative skills for a day. The
site helps both groups understand the invitation, apply, and know what follows.

### Brand Personality
Warm host: practical and plain in logistical details, playful in invitations,
and collaborative in how it describes makers and organisations.

### Aesthetic Direction
A two-colour editorial broadsheet rooted in Brussels, with a poster-like
condensed display face, friendly sans-serif reading type, warm cream, and red.
Keep the existing identity and avoid generic corporate styling.

### Design Principles
- Align related editorial elements precisely to the type grid.
- Use the shared palette, typography, and named design tokens.
- Keep invitations warm and playful; keep practical information direct.
- Present makers and organisations as active partners.
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
