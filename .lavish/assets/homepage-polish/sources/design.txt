0098  # Design System: Make a Mark Brussels
0099  
0100  ## Overview
0101  
0102  **Creative North Star: "The Brussels Broadsheet"**
0103  
0104  The site reads like a two-colour broadsheet printed for the event.
0105  Condensed uppercase headlines in red shout across warm cream paper.
0106  Inset red blocks with rounded corners break the page into editions of the story. All photographs use neutral monochrome and reveal their original color on rollover or activation.
0107  Density is low and the voice is loud: a few big statements, short body copy, generous section spacing.
0108  
0109  The print metaphor sets the limits.
0110  There are two ink families (red and ink-black) on cream paper, with two red surfaces and two cream surfaces creating tonal depth.
0111  Photos sit on the page at slight angles, like clippings, with soft print shadows; everything else is flat.
0112  The hand-drawn Brussels logo is the one human mark among the type.
0113  
0114  **Key Characteristics:**
0115  - Two inks on cream; no third accent colour.
0116  - Condensed uppercase display type at poster scale, set tight (0.92 line height).
0117  - Rounded red blocks inset from the viewport edge, alternating with cream sections.
0118  - Neutral monochrome photography throughout, revealing full colour on hover, tap or keyboard activation.
0119  - Tracked uppercase pills as the only button form.
0120  
0121  ## Colors
0122  
0123  A two-ink print palette: red does the shouting, ink does the reading, cream is the paper.
0124  
0125  ### Primary
0126  - **Marker Red** (`marker-red`): the main ink. Fills the red blocks, the footer, solid pills, the mobile menu button, and the photo-strip focus ring and scrollbar. Text inside a red block is cream.
0127  - **Headline Red** (`headline-red`): a slightly brighter red for display type, numerals, FAQ questions, and the wordmark on cream. It also supplies `--red-surface` for the homepage process-number panels. At 3.45:1 on cream it is for large text only; small cream text does not appear on the lighter red panels.
0128  
0129  ### Neutral
0130  - **Paper Cream** (`paper-cream`): the page background, nav background and display headings inside red blocks.
0131  - **Paper Cream Light** (`paper-cream-light`): body text and outlines inside red blocks (4.78:1 on Marker Red).
0132  - **Paper Cream Inset** (`paper-cream-inset`, `#EBD5BB`): darker cream behind the homepage's two equal audience panels in the refinement preview. It comes from the global site's schedule panels. Ink text has 12.16:1 contrast on it. [Review](docs/wiki/build/12-global-design-review.md)
0133  - **Ink** (`ink`): all body copy on cream (13.7:1), nav links, and the skip link.
0134  - **Duotone Tint** (`duotone-tint`): a dark red wash multiplied over greyscale photos to create the duotone.
0135  - **Rule Red** (`rule-red`): hairline dividers between FAQ items and above the maker steps.
0136  
0137  ### Named Rules
0138  **The Two Inks Rule.** Red and ink on cream, and nothing else. A new colour needs a product reason, not a decorative one.
0139  
0140  **The Large Red Rule.** Headline Red is used for large text and the lighter-red process-number panels. Body-size red text on cream uses Marker Red at minimum, and small cream text sits on the deeper Marker Red. Lighter-red panels carry large numerals only.
0141  
0142  ## Typography
0143  
0144  **Display Font:** Big Shoulders Display (with Impact, sans-serif), self-hosted in weights 300 and 600.
0145  **Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with system-ui, sans-serif), self-hosted in weights 400 and 600.
0146  
0147  **Character:** A condensed, industrial poster face does all the talking.
0148  A friendly, open geometric sans carries the explanations.
0149  
0150  ### Hierarchy
0151  - **Display** (600, `clamp(56px, 9vw, 118px)`, 0.92): the homepage hero is left aligned and capped at 13ch, paired with the photo collage on desktop.
0152  - **Headline** (600, `clamp(40px, 6vw, 78px)`, 0.92): section headings such as "Info for Makers" and "FAQ".
0153  - **Title** (600, `clamp(26px, 3vw, 34px)`, 0.92): step titles. FAQ questions use the same face at `clamp(20px, 2.2vw, 28px)`.
0154  - **Numeral** (300, `clamp(48px, 5vw, 64px)`): zero-padded step and FAQ numbers ("01", "02"), light weight against the semibold titles.
0155  - **Process numeral** (300, `clamp(72px, 7vw, 104px)`, 1): homepage process numbers on lighter-red panels; uses `--fs-process-num`.
0156  - **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): all prose. The hero intro rises to `clamp(16px, 1.4vw, 19px)`. Measure stays between 560 and 680px.
0157  - **Label** (600, 12px, 0.12em tracking, uppercase): kickers, nav links, footer links, and pill text.
0158  
0159  ### Named Rules
0160  **The Shout and Explain Rule.** Display type is always uppercase Big Shoulders. Anything a reader has to understand (instructions, answers, dates) is sentence-case Plus Jakarta Sans.
0161  
0162  **The Light Numeral Rule.** Sequence numbers use the 300 weight, so the semibold title next to them stays the loudest element.
0163  
0164  ## Layout
0165  
0166  The owner-selected distilled homepage pairs its existing headline and two
0167  audience actions with a two-photo collage. At 860px and below, the hero becomes
0168  one column and displays one group image. The hero leads directly to equal
0169  audience panels on darker cream. One red edition block combines date and youth
0170  focus; a Brussels photo with evidence links follows. The homepage has no
0171  process section, story strip or global FAQ. Audience pages carry process,
0172  preparation and cost details. The original remains in the local comparison.
0173  [Selection and implementation](docs/wiki/build/13-messaging-review.md)
0174  
0175  A single centred column with a 1360px container and a fluid gutter (`clamp(20px, 3vw, 40px)`).
0176  Cream sections sit directly on the page.
0177  Red blocks are inset by the block margin on three sides, so cream paper shows around them like a page border.
0178  Sections breathe with `clamp(56px, 8vw, 96px)` vertical padding.
0179  
0180  Grids are simple: the homepage hero uses 1.12fr / 1fr; edition details use two equal columns; the evidence section uses 1.1fr / .9fr with a maximum 600px photo; About uses 1.2fr / 1fr. Both audience panels have equal weight and the same darker-cream surface.
0181  Everything collapses to one column at 860px, and the nav becomes a menu toggle at 760px.
0182  The original comparison retains its horizontally scrolling photo strip; the selected homepage uses one Brussels evidence image.
0183  The sticky nav is about 72px tall, and anchored sections offset their scroll margin to match.
0184  
0185  ## Elevation & Depth
0186  
0187  The page is flat.
0188  Depth appears only on photographs, which behave like printed clippings laid on the paper.
0189  
0190  ### Shadow Vocabulary
0191  - **Clipping** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px rgba(60, 30, 20, 0.18)`): all photographs use the shared `.photo-frame`, with rounded corners and no paper border.
0192  
0193  ### Named Rules
0194  **The Only Photos Lift Rule.** Blocks, pills, tags, and text never cast shadows. If something isn't a photograph, it stays flat on the paper.
0195  
0196  **The Clipping Tilt Rule.** Hero photos rotate -5deg and 4deg on desktop, or -2deg on mobile; strip photos rotate between -2deg and 2deg, and the group photo rotates 3deg. Alternate strip items drop 18px. Revealed photos straighten; reduced-motion users get them straight.
0197  
0198  ## Shapes
0199  
0200  Soft rectangles and full pills.
0201  Red blocks and the footer use the block radius (18px), photo cards the shared card radius (10px), and the skip link and small chrome around 6 to 10px.
0202  Interactive and list elements (pills, organisation tags) are full pills.
0203  Circles appear only as the FAQ disclosure icon and the mobile menu button.
0204  Lines are hairlines: 1px Rule Red for dividers, 1.5px for pill and icon outlines.
0205  
0206  ## Components
0207  
0208  ### Pills (buttons)
0209  Ticket-stub confident: uppercase, tracked, outlined at rest, flooding with ink on hover.
0210  - **Shape:** full pill, 1.5px border, 12px × 20px padding, Label type.
0211  - **Outline on cream:** Marker Red border with ink text. Hover and focus flood with Marker Red and turn the text cream.
0212  - **Outline in a red block:** a cream border in `currentColor`. Hover floods with Paper Cream Light and turns the text red.
0213  - **Solid:** Marker Red fill with cream text, inverting to cream with red text on hover. Used only for the primary action of the moment (Apply while applications are open).
0214  - Pills render through `flux:button` with the `pill` class, and external links open in a new tab.
0215  
0216  ### Red Block
0217  The broadsheet's feature box: Marker Red, 18px radius, inset from the viewport, and padded generously.
0218  Headings inside are Paper Cream, and body text and links are Paper Cream Light.
0219  
0220  ### Photography and photo strip
0221  
0222  The hero currently uses two temporary international photos, recorded with
0223  source URLs in `resources/content/site.json` under `hero.photos`. They are
0224  locally served responsive WebPs and use neutral grayscale with 1.04 contrast,
0225  without a cream/red wash. A visible source caption identifies the global
0226  community. Photo corners use the 10px card radius and the existing Clipping
0227  shadow. The desktop collage tilts -5deg and 4deg; mobile shows one image at
0228  -2deg. Reduced-motion mode removes these rotations. Replace these placeholders
0229  with sourced Brussels photos before public launch. All three homepage photographs share
0230  `<x-photo-reveal>` with a monochrome base, unfiltered color overlay and the
0231  same rounded frame and shadow. [Photo selection](docs/raw/2026-10-18-all-photo-treatment-selection.md); [homepage selection](docs/raw/2026-10-18-messaging-selection.md).
0232  - **Evidence photo:** one Brussels group photo with a 600px maximum width.
0233  - **Original comparison only:** the former edition photo and tilted 9:16 story strip remain in the preserved version.
0234  - **Monochrome and color reveal:** all photos use `grayscale(1) contrast(1.04)` through `.photo--monochrome`. Each reveals an unfiltered original-color overlay with a 700ms expanding reveal, 40px feather, 1.025 zoom and straightening. Click, tap or Enter pins the color image; Escape restores monochrome. Reduced-motion mode removes zoom, tilt and the transition. Without JavaScript, monochrome remains visible. The saved Riso preset belongs to the local tool and is not selected by public homepage content. [Selection and use](docs/wiki/build/11-site-photo-treatments.md).
0235  
0236  ### Organisation Tags
0237  Cream-outlined pills inside a red block, 14px text, wrapping in a 10px-gap flow.
0238  Linked tags underline on hover.
0239  
0240  ### FAQ Disclosure
0241  A native `details` list with Rule Red hairlines above and below.
0242  Each row has a light numeral, an uppercase red question, and a 28px circular plus icon that becomes a minus when the row is open.
0243  Answers indent under the question to 680px wide.
0244  
0245  ### Navigation
0246  A sticky cream bar with the uppercase red wordmark on the left and Label-style ink links on the right.
0247  Links underline on hover and focus with a 4px offset.
0248  Below 760px a 40px Marker Red circle with a cream hamburger opens a full-width cream drop-down.

0250  ## Do's and Don'ts
0251  
0252  ### Do:
0253  - **Do** set every heading in uppercase Big Shoulders Display at 0.92 line height, coloured Headline Red on cream or Paper Cream in a red block.
0254  - **Do** put the most important information for a phase in a red block inset by the block margin with an 18px radius.
0255  - **Do** use the shared photo-reveal component for every public photo, with monochrome styling and the original-color reveal.
0256  - **Do** use the solid pill for at most one primary action per view, and outline pills for everything else.
0257  - **Do** keep body text Ink on cream or Paper Cream Light on red, at 17px with 1.6 line height.
0258  
0259  ### Don't:
0260  - **Don't** add a third interface colour, gradients, or tints beyond the duotone wash. The owner-selected risoprint inks are confined to opted-in photographs.
0261  - **Don't** set body-size text in Headline Red (3.45:1 on cream fails WCAG AA for normal text).
0262  - **Don't** add shadows to blocks, pills, or cards that aren't photographs.
0263  - **Don't** use square or slightly rounded buttons; actions are full pills.
0264  - **Don't** replace the hand-drawn Brussels logo with a typeset version or recolour it.
