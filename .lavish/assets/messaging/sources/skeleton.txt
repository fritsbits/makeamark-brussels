43  ## Same edition home — closed and after-event states
44  
45  ```text
46  SIGNUPS CLOSED: clear status + what happens to applicants
47    [Maker future interest] [Organisation future interest]
48  
49  AFTER EVENT: results and work lead; future interest stays optional
50    [Maker future interest] [Organisation future interest]
51  ```
52  
53  The page boundary and state-driven home are owner-selected. The hero leads with
54  the edition purpose and gives both applicant audiences equal actions. Case
55  studies are deferred until content exists and will each have their own page
56  later. [Structure feedback; owner selected this hierarchy]
57  
58  ## Audience information page pattern
59  
60  ```text
61  NAV / link back to edition
62  WHO IT'S FOR: audience and the kind of challenge/contribution
63  WHAT IT OFFERS: what a useful collaboration can make possible
64  COMMITMENT: time, preparation, assets, and expectations
65  PROCESS: what happens after applying → selection → event
66  FAQ: audience-specific questions
67  CTA: start the dedicated application form
68  ```
69  
70  Maker and organisation pages use the same pattern, with audience-specific
71  content. Exact copy and form fields depend on the reviewer-led field audit.
72  The supplied forms ask different questions and set different commitments.
73  [Airtable forms capture; owner selected this information order]
74  
75  ## Application form skeleton
76  
77  The owner selected a single page with grouped sections and a final review for
78  each application. The reviewer-led field audit determines the final groups and
79  fields; the supplied forms currently group identity, motivation/mission,
80  language, work or challenge, preferences/logistics, and commitment checks.
81  [Airtable forms capture; owner selected this layout]
82  
83  ```text
84  FORM INTRO: edition / audience / estimated commitment
85  GROUPED APPLICATION SECTIONS: fields determined after reviewer audit
86  REVIEW: submitted answers, required items, and any corrections
87  SUBMIT
88  CONFIRMATION: receipt + response timeline only if the team can meet it
89  ```
