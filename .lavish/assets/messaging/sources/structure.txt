17  # Structure
18  
19  ## Proposed site map
20  
21  The owner selected one stable phase-aware edition page, separate maker and
22  organisation information pages with dedicated forms, and a current-edition-only
23  scope for v1. The sitemap below records that direction; final routes are still
24  subject to implementation naming.
25  
26  ```text
27  Make a Mark Brussels
28  ├── Current edition — phase-aware edition hub
29  │   ├── For makers — what to expect and how to apply
30  │   │   └── Maker application
31  │   ├── For organisations — fit, challenge, and commitments
32  │   │   └── Organisation application
33  │   ├── Signups-closed state
34  │   │   ├── Future interest: makers
35  │   │   └── Future interest: organisations
36  │   └── After-event state
37  │       ├── Results and work
38  │       └── Future interest: makers and organisations
39  ├── Case studies — later, one page per case study when content exists
40  ├── About / how it works / FAQ
41  └── Contact
42  ```
43  
44  ### Phase behavior in the structure
45  
46  - The edition hub is the stable entry point for the active edition.
47  - The edition's phase determines which primary action and supporting content
48    the hub presents: application routes while open, future-interest routes when
49    closed, and results plus future interest after the event.
50  - Maker and organisation journeys stay distinct because their forms and
51    commitments differ. [Airtable forms capture]
52  - The migration direction makes Laravel the ongoing system of record; this
53    sitemap does not imply Airtable routes or a website admin. [Scope feedback]
54  
55  ## Evidence and interpretation
56  
57  The current site is a one-page layout with navigation, edition information,
58  maker and organisation sections, stories, FAQ, About, and contact. Its captured
59  edition-status behavior uses open, closed/selected, and done states, with no
60  date-based changes. [Astro source capture]
61  
62  The owner's selected product role is an edition-cycle guide with two co-primary
63  application routes and after-event results. The phase-aware edition hub and
64  two separate audience information pages are owner-selected. The current site
65  remains the source for general About/FAQ/contact content. Case studies are
66  planned as individual pages later, when material is available. [Structure
67  feedback]

69  ## Carried questions
70  
71  - Decide how much of the existing one-page content is retained or reduced when
72    arranging the current edition page and audience information pages.
73  - Case studies need their own page type in a later release; no case-study
74    content is available for v1.
75  - Map the migrated Edition status values to signup open, signups closed, and
76    after-event before relying on phase-driven navigation.
77  - Keep the status-to-phase mapping, privacy, and full data-import inventory
78    open from Scope. [Scope]
