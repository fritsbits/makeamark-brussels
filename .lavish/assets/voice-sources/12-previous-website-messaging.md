# Previous website messaging reference

The owner supplied a five-page Google Docs PDF as content from a previous website. Its text refers to the 2025 edition; the website's original publication date is unknown. The October 2026 PDF metadata dates the export. The [source capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-previous-website-copy.md) preserves all five extracted pages and a separate partial transcription of page 2's image-only collage. ## Messaging to carry forward

Editorial recommendation: describe the exchange in concrete terms. Organisations bring a challenge and knowledge of their work; makers bring creative skills. Together they work on something useful. The older copy supports both social contribution and connection with other creatives; the [validated Strategy](../../ux/_strategy.md) keeps makers and organisations co-primary.

Editorial recommendation: use the old document as an example bank, not a style rulebook. Its movement between "social-profits," "social organisation," and "impact organisation" helps explain the need for the owner's selected terminology. Use **social organisation** first and **organisation** after. [Page 1](../../raw/2026-10-18-previous-website-copy.md#page-1), [owner choice TOV-003](../../raw/2026-10-18-tone-of-voice-feedback-round-1.md)

Editorial recommendation: let warmth come from welcome, appreciation, and accurate practical care. The historical provisions are examples of how the earlier copy made the event feel hosted; they are not confirmed promises for 2027. [Page 4](../../raw/2026-10-18-previous-website-copy.md#page-4)

## What stays historical

The PDF's 2025 date, ten organisation places, Muntpunt venue, selection and preparation timings, seventh-edition count, registration links, organisers, and sponsors describe that source. They need current confirmation before reuse. Application invitations also coexist with "Applications are closed" in the captured document; this is not evidence of the current website phase. [Pages 1, 3, 4 and 5](../../raw/2026-10-18-previous-website-copy.md#full-machine-extracted-text)

The Google Doc joke depends on the old platform. "Top creatives," "look fabulous," and the joke-led payment answer are references to evaluate, not instructions to reinstate. The five confirmed voice decisions remain in force, and TOV-006 to TOV-011 remain pending. [Voice guide](10-tone-of-voice.md#7-owner-decisions-and-copy-calibration)

## Confidence and limits

Confidence is high that these phrases appear in the supplied document. Confidence in their effectiveness is unknown: website copy is not audience research, and most overlaps with the existing site are repetitions within the same organisation's materials, not independent corroboration. The page 2 visual supplement is explicitly partial. Existing concerns about current audience needs, venue, and operational rules remain open or partly resolved in the [concerns register](01-strategy-concerns.md).
