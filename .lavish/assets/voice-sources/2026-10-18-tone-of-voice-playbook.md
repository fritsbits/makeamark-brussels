## Purpose
Create a practical, opinionated tone of voice guide for a client organisation — grounded in who they are, who they serve, and how they currently communicate. The output can immediately be used as a reference when writing copy, generating content, or briefing others.
**Aliases:** tone of voice, TOV guide, communicatiestijl, schrijfstijl gids
---
## Inputs needed
Before running this playbook, gather or confirm the following:
1. **Organisation name and basic mission** — what do they do and for whom?
2. **Target audience** — who are their primary users/customers/beneficiaries?
3. **Key communication channels** — website, social media, newsletter, internal comms?
4. **Links to existing content** — website URL, social media profiles, any existing brand docs
5. **Project context** — why is this guide being created? Rebrand, new website, consistency issues?
---
## Steps
### 1. Research the organisation
- Read their website thoroughly: mission, about page, services/programmes, news
- Scan 10–15 social media posts across their active channels
- Note any existing brand or communication documents if available
- Identify: who they are, what they stand for, who they serve
### 2. Understand the audience
- Define the primary audience(s): demographics, needs, context
- Identify key pain points, hopes, and expectations of that audience
- Note how the audience communicates themselves (formal/informal, Dutch/French/English, etc.)
### 3. Analyse current tone of voice
- Extract 5–10 example sentences from their current communications
- Assess: formal vs informal, active vs passive, warm vs distant, jargon vs plain language
- Identify what’s working and what feels off or inconsistent
### 4. Draft the tone of voice guide
Use the **Tone-of-voice-gids template** as the output structure:
- **TOV statement** — one powerful sentence (≤ 25 words) capturing how and why they communicate
- **Voice attributes** — 5–7 adjectives with explanation and example sentence each
- **Language & style guidelines** — specific do’s and don’ts with rationale
- **Terminology list** — preferred and avoided words/terms
- **Channel adaptations** — tone nuances per channel (website, social, internal, formal)
> 📌 Template reference: [Tone-of-voice-gids – template](https://www.notion.so/2afd3ecc475c80859dc1e866923e70be)
### 5. Save output
- Create a new page in the client’s project folder in Notion titled **“Tone of voice guide – \[Organisation name\]”**
- Populate it using the template structure above
- Flag any assumptions made or sections that need client validation
---
## Expected output
A complete tone of voice guide page in Notion, ready to use as a writing reference — with TOV statement, voice attributes, style rules, terminology, and channel guidance. Should be practical enough that an AI agent or team member can immediately apply it when generating content.

## Notes
- This guide is a starting point — always flag where client validation would strengthen it
- Aim for opinionated and useful over comprehensive and vague
- Dutch is the default language for Flemish non-profit clients unless otherwise specified

#
Dit document helpt je stap voor stap een consistente, praktijkgerichte tone‑of‑voice‑gids op te bouwen.
---
# **1. Tone‑of‑voice‑statement**
### **Doel**
De “noordster” van je communicatie: één krachtige zin die samenvat *hoe* en *waarom* je klinkt.
### **Inhoud**
- Max. 25 woorden
- Actief geformuleerd, tegenwoordige tijd
- Kernwaardes of voice‑attributen benoemd
### **Voorbeelden**
- “Werkmmaat communiceert op mensenmaat: warm, betrokken, transparant en doelgericht – wij maken werk persoonlijk en betekenisvol.”
- “vzw De Rand communiceert helder en betrokken: wij spreken direct, actief en inclusief, met aandacht voor duidelijkheid en maatschappelijke impact.”
---

### **Inhoud**

- Per attribuut: korte uitleg + voorbeeldzin

# **3. Taal‑ & stijlrichtlijnen**
### **Doel**
Praktische regels die elke schrijver (mens of AI) kan toepassen; beperken interpretatie‑risico.
### **Inhoud**
- Specifieke regel
- *Do*‑ en *Don’t*‑voorbeeld
- Waarom dit belangrijk is
### **Voorbeelden**
1. Inclusieve “wij”‑toon
	*Do*: “Wij creëren werk op maat voor iedere unieke situatie.”
	*Don’t*: “Er wordt werk op maat gecreëerd.”
2. Actieve, korte zinnen
	*Do*: “We zetten onze talenten in om samen succes te boeken.”
	*Don’t*: “Onze talenten worden ingezet om succes te boeken.”
---
