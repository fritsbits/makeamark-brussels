Decision 1 · date lockup

Where should the date sit?

Each option removes the redundant Edition link so the date placement stays easy to compare. The calendar icon is shown only where it may help identify a metadata line.

D-A · Recommended

Under the title

Move the full date into the left title block, directly after the headline. Use a small calendar icon and omit the location.

Trade-off: the date becomes part of the maker invitation; the left block needs enough breathing room beneath the three-line title.
