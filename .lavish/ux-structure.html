Open/closed/after-event content can share one edition home. The sitemap separates the two applications and future-interest routes.

Keep the phase views together or split them?

Structure choice ST-001 · Edition lifecycle

The owner wants a guide across three phases. The existing site already changes its edition action by status; the sitemap extends that idea to a stable edition home. [Astro source; Strategy]

Proposed: one phase-aware edition home

Same edition URL; its leading content and action change with its status. Results replace the signup invitation after the event.
