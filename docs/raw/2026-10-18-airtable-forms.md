## Maker application

Screenshot: `.bloom/attachments/TfsUTR/Application-form-for-Makers-Airtable-10-18-2026_09_01_AM.png`.

The form title is “Application form for Makers” and the subtitle asks whether the applicant wants to join on Saturday March 15. Fields are grouped as follows:

- **About you:** required name, required email address, required reason for participating, and where the applicant heard about Make a Mark.
- **Languages:** Dutch, English, French (each shown as a star rating), and another language.
- **About your work:** required portfolio, social media, required main skills, backup skills, and other skills.
- **Preferences for the make-a-thon:** dietary restrictions, team preferences, and optional social themes. Themes shown are poverty reduction and social security; culture and community building; health and well-being; housing and living conditions; local economy and employment; environment and sustainability; education and youth development; social inclusion and diversity; sports and recreation; refugee and migrant support.
- **Practical checks:** required confirmation that the date is in the applicant's calendar; required availability for one or more pre-event planning meeting times; required confirmation that, if accepted, the applicant can attend the full event and pre-event planning meeting.

## Organisation application

Screenshot: `.bloom/attachments/Q3PczD/Application-form-for-organisations-Airtable-10-18-2026_09_00_AM.png`.

The form title is “Application form for organisations” and the subtitle says it is an application for one of ten spots at Make a Mark Brussels (March 15, 2025). Fields are grouped as follows:

- **Time commitment:** required confirmation that the organisation can provide makers with the information and assets needed; required availability for a preliminary planning meeting; required availability for a feedback session during the make-a-thon; required availability for the evening presentation.
- **About the organisation:** required confirmation that it helps people in Brussels; required organisation name and mission; social media accounts; website URL.
- **Challenge:** required description of the creative challenge, with examples including visual identity, a campaign, communications strategy, and social media assets.
- **Contact:** required contact name, email, and phone; language fields for English, Dutch, and French.
