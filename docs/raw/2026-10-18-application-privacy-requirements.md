# Application privacy requirements — official source capture

- Captured: 2026-10-18.
- Purpose: identify what organisational information is missing before writing the application privacy notice.
- Sources: GDPR Article 13 on EUR-Lex and the Belgian Data Protection Authority's information-rights guidance and sensitive-data overview.

## Evidence

When an organisation collects personal data directly from a person, GDPR
Article 13 says it must provide information at collection including controller
identity/contact, DPO contact when applicable, processing purposes and legal
basis, recipients or recipient categories, transfers where applicable, storage
period or its criteria, rights, the right to complain to a supervisory
authority, whether providing data is required and consequences of not doing so,
and automated decision-making where applicable. [GDPR Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)

The Belgian Data Protection Authority describes the information right in
similar terms: people should be told why data is needed, how long it is kept,
who receives it, and the legal basis. It also lists access, correction,
portability, objection, restriction, and deletion rights, depending on the
processing and legal basis. [Belgian DPA: information right](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/professioneel/avg/rechten-van-de-burgers/het-recht-op-informatie)
· [Belgian DPA: data subject rights](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/burger/privacy/wat-zijn-mijn-rechten)

The Belgian DPA identifies health information and religious or philosophical
beliefs among special categories of data under GDPR Article 9. The maker form's
optional free-text dietary requirements field could invite such details, for
example where an applicant volunteers a medical or religious reason. This is a
risk to assess and a reason to collect only the operational information the
event actually needs. [Belgian DPA: sensitive data](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/professioneel/thema-s/gevoelige-gegevens)

## Interpretation and limits

This capture summarises official sources; it is not a legal determination of
the organisation's lawful basis, applicable Article 9 condition, or final
notice. Those depend on the controller, applicant relationship, purposes,
actual fields, recipients, technical setup, and retention plan. A qualified
privacy adviser should review those choices where needed.
