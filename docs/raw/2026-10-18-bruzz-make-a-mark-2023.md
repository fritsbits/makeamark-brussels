## Reported details

The report describes an edition where ten social-profit organisations and creative-sector participants worked together for twelve hours. It presents Make a Mark as matching organisations with people such as designers, developers, illustrators, and photographers, who work in teams on communication and design challenges.

Organisations quoted in the report describe lacking time or resources for design and marketing despite the importance of their work. Examples of outputs include a visual identity, communication plan, colour palette, elevator pitch, logo, social-media plan, and public-facing messages.

Makers quoted in the report describe participating to apply their skills to socially meaningful work, contribute to organisations that may not have the budget to hire creative professionals, meet like-minded people, and experience a close-knit community. One participant says the first event felt overwhelming but that they returned for later editions. The report also mentions that makers often are not asked for further work after the marathon, although some continue projects or relationships voluntarily.

## Limits

These are reported observations and quotations from one 2023 edition, not representative research across all participants or current editions. The source supports hypotheses about motivations, expectations, and possible anxieties; it does not validate them for the next edition.
