# Homepage photo reveal selection

Source: owner messages in the working session, 2026-10-18.

The owner requested a larger homepage photo and several adjustable rollover
options that reveal the original photograph:

> We've got this risoprint styled photo on the homepage. It needs to be bigger. And I want to fine-tune the rollover effect. And effect that lets you go the the original photo. Suggest several options and let me tweak.

After trying the photo rollover playground, the owner selected the effect:

> I love the expanding reveal

This selects the expanding reveal. The owner did not individually select
the numeric settings. The agent applied the playground preset defaults:
600px maximum frame width, 700ms transition, 40px feather, 1.025 original-photo
zoom, and straightening from the existing 3-degree tilt.
