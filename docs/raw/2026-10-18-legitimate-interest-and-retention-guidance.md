# Legitimate interests and retention — official source capture

- Captured: 2026-10-18.
- Purpose: support a preliminary assessment of a lawful basis and a short retention rule for edition applications.

## Evidence

GDPR Article 6(1)(f) permits processing only where it is necessary for the
controller's or a third party's legitimate interests, except where the
person's interests or fundamental rights and freedoms override those
interests. Article 5(1)(e) limits identifiable storage to the time needed for
the processing purpose. [GDPR, Articles 5 and 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)

The European Data Protection Board's Guidelines 1/2024 describe three
cumulative checks for legitimate interests: the interest must be legitimate,
the processing must be necessary for it, and the interests/rights of affected
people must not override it. The controller should assess and document those
checks before relying on Article 6(1)(f). [EDPB Guidelines 1/2024](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-10/edpb_guidelines_202401_legitimateinterest_en.pdf)
