## FAQ (#faq)

1. **Do Makers get paid for the day of work?** "Kind of. In drinks, delicious snacks and good vibes."
2. **Do organisations need to pay to participate?** "Nope. The Makers gift their time and talent to help Brussels. No strings attached."
3. **Is there a Bruzz article with video that can give me a sense of the vibe of the event?** "Yes, there is." https://www.bruzz.be/samenleving/creatieve-marathon-make-mark-maakt-brussel-opnieuw-een-beetje-beter-2023-03-19
