# Organisation invitation direction

Source: owner feedback sent through Lavish on 2026-10-19.

## Verbatim feedback

> The wording for the social organization is off because it implies that the social organization would also do the work. Something like “work with makers” or “tackle a challenge with a maker team” is more like this: you can get help from this team, and they will make it for you. They will make communication or design challenges that you've been waiting a long time to take on but haven't been able to do, and they'll help you bring them to life. It's quite the shift.

This feedback concerns the organisation invitation wording in the [audience arrangement review](../../.lavish/audience-arrange.html). No replacement wording was selected in this message.
