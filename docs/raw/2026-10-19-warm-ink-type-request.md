The owner says the black typography feels bland and asks for a small amount of
cream in the black so it blends with the paper. They suggest a second,
distinctly lighter dark-brown ink for some sections. For the lead paragraph
below “What you commit to,” they request the larger font treatment used by the
maker hero lead, in brown.

The attached screenshot shows the current maker commitment section: a red
display heading, a 17px black paragraph beneath it, and numbered steps to the
right. The request changes the paragraph's type and ink roles; the red heading
and step styles remain as shown.
