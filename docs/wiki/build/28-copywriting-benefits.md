# Audience-led titles and copy

## Decision

How should the public titles make the value of Make a Mark clearer to makers
and social organisations? This page analyses the current wording and proposes
an audience-led revision. The proposals are not approved for publication; no
site copy has been changed.

## What the evidence says

**Current copy.** The homepage headline is “One day. Many ways to make a mark.”
Its supporting line explains that volunteer makers and Brussels social
organisations team up on communication, design, and digital challenges. The
audience cards say “Give your creative skills to a local project and meet other
makers” and “Work with a maker team on one communication, design or digital
challenge.” [Current homepage copy](../../../resources/content/site.json) and
[homepage template](../../../resources/views/home.blade.php).

The maker page title is “A day for your creative skills.” Its lead already
connects using those skills on a local project with meeting other makers. The
organisation page title is “Bring one real communication challenge.” Its lead
says to work with a team of makers and names possible work types. [Audience
page](../../../resources/views/audience.blade.php); the maker lead was
requested by the owner on 19 October ([request](../../raw/2026-10-19-maker-hero-lead-request.md)).

**Owner direction.** The owner says the organisation invitation should make
the division of work clear: the organisation brings a long-deferred
communication or design project; a maker team helps bring it to life. The
current phrase “work with a team” can suggest shared execution and misses the
benefit of getting dedicated creative help. [Owner direction](../../raw/2026-10-19-organisation-invitation-direction.md).

**Motivation evidence.** A 2023 BRUZZ report describes makers seeking socially
meaningful use of their skills and connection with like-minded peers. It
reports that some organisations lacked time or resources for design and
marketing. These are historical observations from one edition, not validated
needs for every current applicant. [BRUZZ source capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-bruzz-make-a-mark-2023.md).

**Voice and product constraints.** Makers and organisations are co-primary
audiences. The owner selected a warm host voice, playful invitations with
plain practical copy, “social organisation” terminology, and an English-first
guide. [Voice guide](../strategy/10-tone-of-voice.md) and [validated
Strategy](../../ux/_strategy.md). The current application page describes the
organisation outcome more cautiously as working towards a “practical direction
or first materials”; titles should not promise a finished deliverable or
outcome. [Audience page](../../../resources/views/audience.blade.php).

## Analysis

The homepage headline is memorable but atmospheric. It names neither audience
nor the useful exchange, so the supporting line has to do all the explanatory
work. The maker title is inviting but generic: “a day” and “creative skills” do
not say that the work can matter to a local organisation, and peer connection
appears only in the lead. The organisation title identifies an input
(“challenge”) but not the benefit (a maker team taking on work that has been
waiting) or who will make it.

The strongest copy structure is therefore different for each audience while
making one shared exchange visible: makers contribute creative skill and gain
meaningful work and peer connection; organisations bring a deferred challenge
and receive focused help from makers. That is an editorial interpretation of
the owner direction and historical evidence, not a tested claim about current
audience preferences.

## Proposed direction

| Surface | Current | Recommendation / owner selection | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage title | One day. Many ways to make a mark. | **Selected: Design and creativity are for everyone.** | The owner chose this custom line. It broadens the invitation; the existing deck explains who takes part. |
| Homepage supporting line | Volunteer makers and Brussels social organisations team up for one day to work on communication, design and digital challenges. | **Makers bring creative skills. Social organisations bring a challenge worth taking on. Together, they make practical work for Brussels.** | Makes the exchange legible while keeping both audiences visible. “Practical work” avoids promising a finished product. |
| Maker page title | A day for your creative skills. | **Current: Use your creative skills to make a difference.** | The owner asked to shorten the title, then selected this wording. |
| Maker page lead | Use your creative skills on a local project and meet other makers. Your team could work on a visual identity, a campaign, communications strategy or digital materials. | **Join a team of makers to work on a communication, design or digital challenge from a Brussels social organisation.** | Moves from the title's impact promise to the collaboration and challenge, without repeating “creative skills.” |
| Organisation page title | Bring one real communication challenge. | **Selected: Get a maker team to bring a long-waiting project to life.** | Puts the organisation benefit and maker team's role up front, following the owner's explicit direction. |
| Organisation page lead | Work with a team of makers on one challenge. It could be a visual identity, a campaign, communications strategy or social media assets. Together, you'll work towards a practical direction or first materials. | **Share a communication, design or digital challenge. Makers will help shape it into a practical direction or first materials.** | Avoids repeating the title's long-waiting project idea; clarifies what the organisation brings and keeps the outcome within the current page's stated scope. |

“A project that matters” and “long-waiting” are framing choices, not proven
motivations or promises. The selected homepage line is the owner's custom
wording; its breadth relies on the existing supporting line to explain the
event.

## Owner selection and implementation

The owner selected **“Design and creativity are for everyone”** for the
homepage, and selected the recommended maker and organisation titles above.
The exact selection is preserved in the [Lavish response capture](../../raw/2026-10-19-copywriting-benefits-selection.md).
The homepage and organisation titles retain that selection. The owner later
asked to shorten the maker title while keeping its meaning; it now reads “Use
your creative skills on work that matters.” Supporting copy remains unchanged.
[Revision request](../../raw/2026-10-19-maker-hero-title-shortening-request.md).
The selected homepage title is also present on the running homepage.

## Alternatives to compare

For the homepage, a more explicit but less warm option is **Creative skills
for real challenges.** For makers, a more concrete option is **Make useful
work with a Brussels social organisation.** For organisations, a more direct
option is **Let a maker team take on your communication challenge.** These
alternatives explain the proposition quickly; the recommended set carries
more of the desired human motivation.

## Limits and open questions

- The maker motivations and organisation resource barriers come from a single
  historical news report; current audience research could confirm or revise
  them. [BRUZZ capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-bruzz-make-a-mark-2023.md)
- The 2027 project capture describes usable results, but its Notion verification
  state is “unverified.” Keep public copy within the current page's narrower
  “practical direction or first materials” wording until outcome commitments
  are confirmed. [2027 capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-notion-make-a-mark-2027.md);
  [current page wording](../../../resources/views/audience.blade.php).
- These recommendations concern message hierarchy and wording only. They do
  not change the approved audience structure, signup scope, fees, or promised
  deliverables. [Strategy](../../ux/_strategy.md); [audience page](../../../resources/views/audience.blade.php).

## Audience lead copy update

After reviewing the titles in context, the owner asked to reduce repetition in
both audience-page leads. The makers lead now explains the collaboration and
the kind of challenge, leaving the title to carry the impact promise. The
organisations lead names the challenge the organisation brings and the bounded
result makers can help shape. [Owner request](../../raw/2026-10-19-audience-lead-copy-request.md);
[template](../../../resources/views/audience.blade.php).

The exact organisation wording is an implementation choice made under the
owner's direction to apply the same treatment; it has not been separately
reviewed. The homepage, makers, and organisations routes were then checked in
the running Laravel app. Each serves its selected title and the revised lead
copy. The wording stays within the organisation page's current outcome scope.
