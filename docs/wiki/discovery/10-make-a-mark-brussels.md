## What it is

A make-a-thon: creatives give one day of voluntary design work to Brussels social-profits.
Running since 2016, "hundreds of creatives" (site claim, not independently verified).
Part of the global Make a Mark movement (letsmakeamark.org), which also exists in "tens of cities".

## Format

- Friday evening: kickoff, planning, getting to know each other.
- All day Saturday: teams work on a creative challenge for the central case. Organisers provide WiFi, food and drinks.
- Makers are selected in advance to form effective teams. Notified end of January. Team and organisation meet in a one-hour evening meeting in the first week of March.
- Makers are unpaid ("drinks, snacks and good vibes"). Organisations pay nothing.

## 2026 edition (eighth)

- 20 and 21 March 2026, "Make a Mark for the Planet".
- Central case: Klimaatzaak / L'Affaire Climat. Theme: climate communication.
- The site speaks in the past tense and announces no next edition.
