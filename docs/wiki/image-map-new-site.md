# New-site image slot map

The approved Skeleton defines one past-edition evidence photo. The homepage
also has two hero photo positions and an event carousel. Its first slide keeps
the 2026 group photo; ten landscape archive photos follow in a consistent
4:3 display frame. The owner requested the bright workshop audience photo
second, the meal-preparation photo fourth and the dark-room audience photo
last; other archive photos retain their relative order.
The makers and organisations UX specs do not define image slots.
[UX Structure](../ux/_structure.md); [UX Skeleton](../ux/_skeleton.md);
[temporary-photo record](build/12-global-design-review.md#initial-homepage-implementation)

The owner selected the two mapped hero photos to replace the temporary global
images, and they are now used on the homepage. Their filenames identify the
Make a Mark 2020 archive; individual photographer credits and publication
rights remain unverified. The existing 2026 group photo leads the carousel and
remains the current photo in the 2027 edition record.
