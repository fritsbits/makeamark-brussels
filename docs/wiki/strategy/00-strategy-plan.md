# Strategy plan

## Purpose

Define why the website exists, who it serves, what they need, how those needs
support the owner's goals, and what measurable behavior would indicate success.

## Current status

The Strategy plane is settled. Scope, Structure, and Skeleton choices were
recorded on 2026-10-18. UX planning stopped at Skeleton; the owner has since
authorized Surface implementation, and the Build phase is underway. The
owner selected all five site-direction choices in `.lavish/ux-strategy.html`.
The validated synthesis is [docs/ux/_strategy.md](../../ux/_strategy.md).
Discovery's remaining Partly concerns and the draft voice guide's pending
choices stay visible as carried context; they do not change the owner-selected
site direction. Scope owner choices are recorded in
[docs/ux/_scope.md](../../ux/_scope.md); migration completeness, phase mapping,
privacy, and detailed field rules carry forward. The owner selected the sitemap
direction through `.lavish/ux-structure.html`. Structure and Skeleton
decisions are recorded in
[docs/ux/_structure.md](../../ux/_structure.md) and
[docs/ux/_skeleton.md](../../ux/_skeleton.md). Surface implementation follows
the approved hierarchy without reopening those choices.

## Method

1. Review the evidence in the Discovery synthesis and source captures.
2. Draft the Strategy plane as a reviewable overview, separating evidence,
   interpretation, and hypotheses.
3. Record the owner's audience, site role, success measure, and after-event
   choices in the validated Strategy plane.
4. Keep direct user research, targets, venue, voice preferences, and data
   handling limits explicit as carried questions.
5. Open Scope only after the Strategy decisions are recorded.

## Completion gate

- A working one-sentence digital mission grounded in owner choices.
- Maker and organisation signup are identified as co-primary; maker is the
  person-signup audience for v1.
- Jobs-to-be-done are clearly identified as hypotheses where evidence is
  historical.
- Candidate North Star is selected by the owner; reviewer definition and
  targets remain open.
- Edition-cycle guide role and after-event priority are selected by the owner.
- Website admin remains later scope.
