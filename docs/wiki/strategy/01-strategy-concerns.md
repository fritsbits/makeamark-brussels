## Open voice copy choices

These editorial refinements do not reopen the settled Strategy plane. The [round 2 review](11-voice-copy-review.md) and `.lavish/tone-of-voice.html` offer six A/B comparisons; none is preselected. The five direction choices above were explicitly submitted in [round 1 feedback](../../raw/2026-10-18-tone-of-voice-feedback-round-1.md).

| ID | Concern | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TOV-006 | What headline rhythm fits the voice? | Compare short active sentences with a conversational invitation. |
| TOV-007 | What should lead a maker invitation? | Compare useful contribution first with connection first, keeping both in the copy. The [previous website reference](12-previous-website-messaging.md) contains both themes; it does not settle their order. |
| TOV-008 | How should an organisation invitation open? | Compare a reader's question with an active invitation. |
| TOV-009 | What sort of playful language feels right? | Compare a playful image with a gentler conversational wink in social copy. |
| TOV-010 | How much welcome belongs before a practical instruction? | Compare the instruction alone with a welcoming sentence first. |
| TOV-011 | How much appreciation belongs in a payment answer? | Compare a factual answer with the same answer plus thanks. |
