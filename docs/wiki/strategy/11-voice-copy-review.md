# Voice copy review, round 2

Six pairs of candidate copy test the boundaries of the [v0.2 guide](10-tone-of-voice.md). The owner chose a warm host voice, playful invitations with plain practical copy, social organisation as the term, an English-first guide, and explaining the youth focus without directly inviting young people. [Round 1 feedback](../../raw/2026-10-18-tone-of-voice-feedback-round-1.md)

All copy below is newly written for this review. No variant is recommended or preselected. These are writing samples, not approved website copy or changes to product scope. Choose A or B, or ask for a mix or rewrite in [Lavish](../../../.lavish/tone-of-voice.html). A note about the words you liked helps distinguish the intended preference from other differences between the variants.

Use each choice as evidence of a preference in that context. Do not turn one choice into a universal rule. If choices conflict, propose a narrower rule and another example instead of guessing.

## TOV-006: Homepage headline

**A**

> Bring a challenge. Bring creative skills. Make something useful together.

**B**

> Let's bring challenges and creative skills together to make something useful.

Shared supporting copy:

> Make a Mark brings volunteer makers and Brussels social organisations together for a day of creative work.

What this helps refine: short, active sentences versus a more conversational invitation. Preserve the event explanation whichever headline wins. The shared proposition draws on the [2027 brief](../../raw/2026-10-18-notion-make-a-mark-2027.md).

## TOV-007: Invitation to makers

**A**

> Put your creative skills to work on a Brussels social organisation's challenge. Spend the day making something useful with other makers.

**B**

> Meet other makers and spend the day creating together. Put your creative skills to work on a Brussels social organisation's challenge.

Shared action: **Apply as a maker**.

What this helps refine: lead with useful contribution or connection with peers. Both variants keep the same opportunities; this is an editorial emphasis test, not a choice of website audience priority. These motivations appear in the [historical BRUZZ capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-bruzz-make-a-mark-2023.md), without proving what current applicants prefer.

## TOV-008: Invitation to organisations

**A**

> Have a design or communication challenge you'd like to work on? Tell us about it in your organisation's application.

**B**

> Bring a design or communication challenge you'd like to work on. Tell us about it in your organisation's application.

Shared action: **Apply as an organisation**.

What this helps refine: opening with a reader's question versus an active invitation. Both give the organisation agency and avoid promising a particular deliverable. Challenge descriptions are grounded in the [organisation application capture](../../raw/2026-10-18-airtable-forms.md).

## TOV-009: A social invitation

**A**

> Give your creative brain a day out. Use your skills with other makers on a Brussels social organisation's challenge.

**B**

> Your creative skills could use some company. Join other makers for a day working on a Brussels social organisation's challenge.

Shared action: **Apply as a maker**.

What this helps refine: a playful image versus a gentler, conversational wink. Both sit within the approved boundary of playful invitations. They are not proposed wording for instructions, errors, or rejection messages. The format and volunteer creative contribution come from the [2027 brief](../../raw/2026-10-18-notion-make-a-mark-2027.md).

## TOV-010: Before applying

**A**

> Before you apply, check that you can attend the event and any required preparation meetings.

**B**

> We'd love to have you with us. Before you apply, check that you can attend the event and any required preparation meetings.

What this helps refine: whether practical copy needs a welcoming sentence before the instruction. Both keep the same requirement and avoid jokes. The [historical forms](../../raw/2026-10-18-airtable-forms.md) include preparation commitments; the current schedule must be confirmed before publishing either variant.

## TOV-011: Payment FAQ

Question: **Do makers get paid?**

**A**

> No. Makers volunteer their time and creative skills.

**B**

> No. Makers volunteer their time and creative skills. We're grateful for the work they put in.

What this helps refine: a concise factual answer versus the same answer with appreciation. Either keeps unpaid participation explicit, without promising food, reimbursements, or other benefits. Volunteer creative work appears in the [2027 brief](../../raw/2026-10-18-notion-make-a-mark-2027.md); any participation fee or reimbursement terms need separate confirmation.

## How choices will change the guide

The subsequently supplied [previous website PDF](12-previous-website-messaging.md) adds historical reference for these comparisons, particularly useful contribution, peer connection, and practical warmth. Its addition does not select a variant or change the six pairs already offered for review.

For each submitted ID, record the chosen wording and any note, then propose a rule for that context. Mark the inference as provisional unless the owner explicitly confirms the rule. Add selected samples to the guide's example bank with their decision IDs. Keep unselected questions open; do not silently treat a skipped question as approval.

Update the guide, [concerns register](01-strategy-concerns.md), index, and log together. A copy preference does not revise the recorded S-001 to S-005 decisions, establish an attendance requirement, or authorise publishing candidate copy to the live website.
