The eighth edition of our make-a-thon for Brussels was a special one, placing climate communication at the centre: we Made a Mark for the Planet.

Our central case was [Klimaatzaak / L'Affaire Climat / Climate Case](https://klimaatzaak.eu/en/), a landmark climate case with impact far beyond Belgium. The climate crisis remains an existential challenge, yet it is increasingly pushed to the background. We asked Makers for their brainpower to break through that trend.
